# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Welcome different backgrounds and skill levels. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing and sharing confidential customer material are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation or ban repeated or serious violations. Apply decisions consistently and consider context and impact.

For non-sensitive moderation concerns, contact a repository maintainer through their publicly listed GitHub contact channel. Do not post private evidence in an issue. Before accepting public community participation, the repository owner must publish a monitored private reporting contact here. No reporting address is invented by this starter.
